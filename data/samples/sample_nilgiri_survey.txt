Ethnobotanical Documentation of Indigenous Medicinal Flora in the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve.
Authors: Dr. S. K. Ramanathan, Irula Traditional Council.
Publication Year: 2023.

In the lower elevations of the Nilgiri Hills, traditional healers extensively utilize Azadirachta indica (Vembu).
The leaves are freshly crushed into a paste and applied topically for wound healing and bacterial infections.
For intermittent malarial fever, the bitter aerial parts of Andrographis paniculata (Nilavembu) are boiled as a decoction and taken orally twice a day.
Additionally, the dried rhizome powder of Zingiber officinale is boiled with water for dyspepsia and acute digestive complaints.
