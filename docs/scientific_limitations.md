# Scientific Limitations & Ethical Principles

## 1. No Medical Treatment or Dosage Advice
EthnoTrace is an academic and pharmacognostic research intelligence tool designed for ethnobotanists, natural product chemists, and historians. **It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or dosage instructions.** Documented traditional uses represent cultural and historical observations, not clinical efficacy or safety proof.

## 2. Respect for Indigenous & Traditional Knowledge
- Community provenance is preserved wherever available.
- Sensitive or confidential sacred plant gathering coordinates are abstracted to broad district boundaries to prevent biopiracy and exploitation.

## 3. Computational Predictions vs Experimental Proof
Computational similarity matches, NLP extractions, and knowledge graph links are treated as working hypotheses. The platform clearly marks algorithmic certainty metrics and requires human-in-the-loop validation for clinical or pharmacopoeial decision-making.

## 4. Zero Fabricated Citations
The Research Copilot operates under strict citation grounding rules. If a queried concept is not present within the indexed primary documents or verified database records, the system explicitly reports that the available repository does not establish the answer, rather than generating synthetic references.
