# Scientific Data Sources & Attributions

EthnoTrace complies with open science standards and attributes external biodiversity and biomedical databases:

### 1. Plants of the World Online (POWO)
- **Provider**: Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew
- **URL**: https://powo.science.kew.org/
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- **Attribution**: Facilitated by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew. Published on the Internet.
- **Fields Utilized**: Accepted scientific names, taxonomic rank, authorship, native range, and IPNI identifiers.

### 2. Medicinal Plant Names Services (MPNS)
- **Provider**: Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew
- **URL**: https://mpns.science.kew.org/
- **License**: Kew Scientific Licensing / CC-BY
- **Attribution**: MPNS Portal, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.
- **Fields Utilized**: Medicinal plant synonyms, pharmacopoeial monographs, and pharmaceutical naming standards.

### 3. Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)
- **Provider**: GBIF Secretariat
- **URL**: https://www.gbif.org/
- **License**: CC0 / CC BY 4.0
- **Attribution**: GBIF Backbone Taxonomy, International Biodiversity Facility.
- **Fields Utilized**: Species identifiers, taxonomic hierarchies, and occurrences.

### 4. PubMed Central & NCBI E-Utilities
- **Provider**: National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)
- **URL**: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
- **License**: Open Access / Public Domain US Gov
- **Attribution**: National Library of Medicine, Bethesda, MD.
- **Fields Utilized**: Peer-reviewed abstracts, PMIDs, DOIs, study designs, and clinical trial outcomes.

### 5. Indian Himalayan Ethnobotanical Dataset
- **Provider**: Synthesized from 2,283 primary studies covering 146 Himalayan districts
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.ethnohimalaya
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
- **Attribution**: Indian Himalayan Ethnobotanical Synthesis, Dryad Data Repository.
- **Fields Utilized**: District-level traditional uses, plant parts, preparations, ethnic communities, and non-sensitive geographic coordinates.
